Hats are only for show. Your progress saves on this device.

Drag the joystick to move and hold FIRE to shoot. WASD or the arrow keys and space work too. Hedges block movement and locust sight. Every 20th level has a boss.

Controller: left stick or d-pad moves, right stick aims (otherwise it aims at the nearest locust), A or a trigger fires, X heals, Y opens hats, Start opens the shop and pauses. In a menu: d-pad to choose, A to buy, B or Start to close, bumpers to switch tabs. Hold Back to return to Bloodline.
